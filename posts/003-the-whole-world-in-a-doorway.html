Chapter Three

The Whole World in a Doorway

We are about to give some very ordinary things more exact names.

Exact language can create a strange illusion. The names get longer, so the world behind them seems to get larger.

It does not.

There may come a point in this book when words like Transformation Pattern, Completion Topology and Persistence Mode look as though we have wandered into a different subject.

That reaction would be understandable.

So before the language becomes precise, let us look at how small the world actually is.

We do not need a laboratory.

We do not need a theorem.

We do not even need to leave the room.

A door will do.

The door is closed.

You hardly have to think about it.

The relevant difference has become determinate at one particular thing: this door. And what remains available is the determination itself.

Differentiation × Focal × Standing

The door closed.

Almost the same words. Almost the same world.

But now you have been given an event. A process that was live reaches its completion at this door, and the completed closing can alter what happens next.

Settlement × Focal × Efficacy

That reading is not produced by the past tense. It comes from the completed intelligibility.

Put them beside each other.

The door is closed.
The door closed.

The furniture of the world has barely moved.

Yet the intelligibility has.

That is why the combinations matter.

The latch keeps the door closed.

Now the closed condition is not simply standing there by itself. It is intelligible through a bearing: latch and door belong together in the result.

Take away the bearing and you no longer have the same grasp.

Relating × Focal × Bearing

The catch on the latch can be used to keep the door from closing.

A possibility has become available at a particular device.

What remains is not merely a fact about the catch. It is something that can now be done.

Opening × Focal × Efficacy

The door fits the frame like a glove.

This time the bearing is not exhausted at one point.

The fit has to hold through the relevant correspondence of door and frame. No single edge or corner is enough by itself.

Relating × Distributed × Bearing

The door begins to scrape along the floor.

You adjust the hinge until it swings cleanly again.

Something was failing relative to an operative criterion. The adjustment makes the fault stop governing the result.

That is Repair.

Now look at the hinges, latch, frame and door together.

Each part has a role it does not have alone. Fitted together, they become one working assembly.

That is Integration.

The whole is not another object placed beside the parts. It is what the parts become able to be together.

Suppose a rule required this door to remain locked.

Then the rule is lifted.

The important thing is not that something negative was said. Something that had governed the situation has lost its determining force.

That is Release.

We have hardly moved.

One door. One frame. A hinge. A latch. A rule.

And already we have touched most of the primitive vocabulary of the system.

The formal world is smaller than the doorway.

There are three questions underneath the grammar.

Transformation Pattern: what kind of unresolvedness changed?

Completion Topology: what structural condition made that transformation count as complete?

Persistence Mode: how does the achieved completion remain available?

Those are the three dimensions.

The first question has six possible answers.

Differentiation. A difference becomes determinate enough to matter.

Opening. Availability, reach, access or possibility becomes greater.

Relating. A bearing between things becomes constitutive.

You already met all three at the door.

And the other three:

Repair. A fault or misfit stops governing the result.

Integration. Separate parts acquire roles in a whole.

Settlement. A live demand for further uptake, choice, completion or continuation is discharged.

Six Transformation Patterns. No seventh is waiting behind the terminology.

The second question has three possible answers.

Focal. One privileged locus is constitutive of completion.

“The door is closed.” This door is enough for the relevant determination.

Completion gathers here.

Distributed. No single locus is sufficient by itself.

“The door fits the frame like a glove.” The fit belongs across the relevant correspondence.

Its plurality is not decorative. It is part of what makes the completion complete.

Completion has to hold through the spread.

Release. Something that previously governed the situation loses its determining force.

The rule requiring the door to remain locked is lifted.

Release is not merely absence, negation, separation or movement away.

It is de-governance.

The third question also has three possible answers.

Standing. The achieved determination itself remains available.

The door is closed.

The result can simply stand.

Bearing. The result remains what it is through some constitutive relation, dependency, condition, frame, source or support.

The latch keeps the door closed.

The bearing belongs to the achieved result.

Efficacy. The result remains as an accomplished capacity, operative effect, changed continuation, rule, function or consequential difference in what can happen.

The catch can keep the door from closing.

Something is now operatively possible.

That is the primitive vocabulary.

Three definitions tell us what the dimensions are. Twelve definitions tell us the values those dimensions can take.

Fifteen primitive definitions altogether.

Nothing needs to be made of that number. It is simply the size of the vocabulary.

And the values combine.

One of six Transformation Patterns.

One of three Completion Topologies.

One of three Persistence Modes.

6 × 3 × 3 = 54

Fifty-four can sound larger than it is.

But you do not learn fifty-four unrelated things.

You learn a very small set of distinctions, and then you learn to feel what happens when they constrain one another inside one completion.

The combinations are natural because their ingredients are already familiar.

Eventually you should not have to think:

Which Transformation Pattern? Which Topology? Which Persistence Mode?

You should be able to feel that a difference became operative, that it had to hold across a field, and that what remains is a constitutive bearing.

Then the formal name merely catches up.

Differentiation × Distributed × Bearing

There is one thing not to do.

Do not turn the dimensions into labels for grammar.

A state is not automatically Standing. An event is not automatically Efficacy. A negative sentence is not automatically Release.

The coordinate belongs to the completed intelligibility, not to the surface appearance of the sentence.

That is why the doorway mattered.

“The door is closed.”

“The door closed.”

“The latch keeps the door closed.”

“The door fits the frame like a glove.”

The words stay close while the completed intelligibilities move through very different parts of the space.

You have now seen the whole primitive world.

This was not a simplified version for beginners.

Later chapters will make the distinctions more exact, combine them more carefully, and test them against much harder material.

But the primitive vocabulary does not suddenly expand behind your back.

Six transformations.

Three ways completion can take shape.

Three ways the achieved completion can remain available.

That is enough to generate the fifty-four.

Next, we let the whole field appear.
