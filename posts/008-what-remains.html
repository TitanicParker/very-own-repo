Episode Eight

What Remains

Episode Seven widened the question until it touched other minds, old proofs, mistakes, books and futures.

What future did a completed shape make available?

Before we answer that at scale, notice how differently a completion can survive even inside one ordinary journey.

Imagine you are standing at an airport gate with a boarding pass in your hand.

It is a small object covered in ordinary marks: your name, a flight number, a seat, a time, a square of code.

The paper is one thing. What has become available through it is not one thing.

You look down and see:

14A.

A minute ago you did not know which seat was yours. Now one place has become determinate enough to use.

Standing

The determination can remain available while nothing happens.

You can walk away from the gate, buy coffee, return twenty minutes later and still know where to sit.

The result itself stands.

But the pass also says that this seat belongs to this passenger on this flight on this date.

14A is not simply floating in the world. Its achieved significance holds through a set of constitutive relations.

Bearing

If you changed the passenger, the flight or the date, you might still see the characters 14A.

You would not have the same completed result.

Some achievements remain what they are through what they bear upon.

Then boarding begins.

You place the pass under a scanner. The gate opens and you walk through.

Something has become operatively possible because the relevant completion is now in force.

Efficacy

The gate does not have to keep opening forever.

The operative difference can exist before it is exercised, and an accomplished consequence can remain after the motion stops.

A completion can remain through what it makes possible.

One boarding pass.

One tiny patch of a day.

Yet three importantly different kinds of future have become visible.

There it is.
It holds through this.
Now this can happen.

Those are the three Persistence Modes we met much earlier.

Standing: the achieved determination itself remains available. Bearing: the result remains what it is through a constitutive relation or condition. Efficacy: the result remains as an accomplished capacity, effect or consequential difference.

The names are not new. Their weight is.

Standing can look static, but it is not dead.

A measured length, an identified person, an established date or a chosen seat can organize later action simply by remaining determinate.

Something need not be moving in order to be load-bearing.

Bearing can look like a vague word for context. It is more exact than that.

The weather outside the terminal may matter to your day. It is not what makes 14A your seat on this flight.

Bearing belongs to what the achieved result is.

Efficacy can look like visible activity. It is not limited to activity.

A repaired brake can sit still and remain usable. A permission can remain operative before anybody acts on it.

Efficacy is achieved consequence, not perpetual motion.

And these are not three stages every completion travels through.

We did not watch Standing mature into Bearing and then graduate into Efficacy. We watched three different completed intelligibilities become available through the same familiar object.

Persistence belongs to the completion itself.

Episode Seven asked what future a shape made available.

Now the question has begun to differentiate itself.

A determination can remain. A result can remain through a relation. A capacity or consequence can alter what may happen next.

There is no single generic way for completion to have a future.

And that creates a problem the boarding pass cannot solve for us.

If the future depends on what actually became complete, then the visible words or marks are not always enough to tell us what happened.

The same expression can appear before a route, after a route, or inside a later one.

The sentence is not the landing.
