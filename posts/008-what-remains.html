Swipe for more

Episode Eight

What Remains

Episode Seven left us with a question that could not have meant quite the same thing at the beginning of this book.

What future did that shape make available?

We already have part of the vocabulary for answering it.

Standing. Bearing. Efficacy.

We met those words at the doorway, and then again when the whole field appeared.

But a definition can arrive before its weight does.

So let us go back even farther.

There is a small brass key on a table.

You have met this key before.

Suppose there are several keys on the table and you ask:

“Which one opens the back door?”

I point.

“The small brass one.”

If the description is enough, one thing has become determinate.

You no longer need to keep all the keys equally alive.

You can reach for this one.

What remains is the achieved determination itself.

You could leave the room and come back a minute later.

The speaker need not point again if you can still identify the key.

The completed difference can simply remain available as the result.

Standing

Now ask a different question.

“How do we know this is the back-door key?”

Someone tries it in the lock.

It fits.

Something new has become intelligible.

Not merely this key.

The result belongs to a relation between key and lock.

If you remove that bearing, you no longer have the same completed grasp.

What remains is this result through that relation.

The key can sit untouched on the table.

The lock can remain in the door.

The relation does not have to be performed every second to remain constitutive of what was achieved.

Bearing

Now put the key in the lock and turn it.

The bolt moves.

The door can open.

The field of what can happen has changed.

The important result is not only that a key was identified, or that a fit was established.

Something has been accomplished.

There is now an operative capacity or consequential difference in what can happen next.

The completion remains through what it can now do.

The door does not have to keep swinging.

A capacity can persist while unused. A rule can persist while no case is before it. An effect can persist after the action that produced it has stopped.

Efficacy

One key.

Three completed intelligibilities.

And three importantly different things left available.

There it is.
It holds through this.
Now this can happen.

This is the third dimension of the grammar becoming easier to feel.

Persistence Mode specifies how the achieved completion remains available for subsequent uptake.

Not how long it lasts.

Not whether someone remembers the words.

What kind of achieved reality is now available to be inherited?

Standing is easy to underestimate because it can look static.

But a standing result is not dead.

Once the right key has been distinguished, that determination can organize the next action. Once a route has been identified, you can take it. Once a choice has settled, you can begin from the choice.

A determination can be load-bearing without being an action.

Bearing is easy to blur into “context.”

But not every surrounding fact belongs to the completed result.

The metal content of the key may be interesting. The weather outside may matter later. Neither is what makes this key fits this lock the result it is.

Bearing is constitutive, not merely nearby.

Efficacy is easy to confuse with visible activity.

But efficacy does not require perpetual motion.

A repaired brake can sit still and remain usable. A permission can remain operative before anyone acts on it. A completed proof step can change what is now lawful to establish.

Efficacy is achieved consequence.

And these are not three stages through which every completion travels.

We did not begin with Standing, advance to Bearing, and graduate into Efficacy.

We made three different completed intelligibilities visible with one familiar object.

Persistence belongs to the completion itself.

You have seen this before at the doorway.

The door is closed.

The achieved determination can stand.

Standing

The latch keeps the door closed.

The closed condition is intelligible through a constitutive bearing.

Bearing

The catch can keep the door from closing.

A capacity has become operatively available.

Efficacy

When those sentences first appeared, we needed them to reveal the primitive vocabulary.

Now they can reveal something else.

They do not merely occupy different places in a table.

They leave different kinds of future behind them.

This changes how the fifty-four can be imagined.

Each form is not only a way something becomes enough.

Because the completed result persists in some determinate mode, each form also constrains what can be inherited from that completion.

A form of completion is also a form of availability.

That does not mean the future is mechanically dictated by the cell.

A standing result can be ignored. A bearing can be forgotten. A capacity can go unused. New conditions can arrive. A completion can later be reopened.

But the next field is no longer empty.

Something specific has been left there.

Episode Seven asked what future a shape made available.

We can now give the beginning of an answer.

Not one generic future.

A determination may stand. A result may remain through a bearing. An achieved capacity or consequence may alter what can happen.

And that gives us a new problem.

If the thing that matters is what actually became complete and what it left available, then the words on the page cannot always tell us enough by themselves.

The same words can appear in very different inherited fields.

Perhaps the sentence is not the landing.

What remains?

Now we know that this question has more than one shape.
