First-contact ledger

The interesting history is not merely the eventual total. It is the sequence: when the first new place appears, when another country joins, when a reader reaches a depth nobody had reached before.

NowPublic spread ledger prepared.waiting

The public spread layer is designed around aggregate events: arrival, voluntary place declaration, first appearance of a place, and reading depth. It should never require a name, email address, precise location, or a permanent cross-site identity. The point is to watch an idea move through the world, not to watch individual people.
