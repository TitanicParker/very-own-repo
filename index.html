Swipe for more

Welcome.

This book is designed to go forward with you.

Swipe left for the next gulp. On a keyboard, the right arrow does the same thing.

Your place is remembered automatically as you read.

The small number in the upper right tells you where you are in this part of the reading.

Episodes takes you back to the entrance whenever you want to jump somewhere else.

That is the whole interface. You can forget it now.

You may notice quite early that this book is unusually clear. That is not an accident, and it is not simply a matter of style.

The book is using the thing it is trying to show you.

You are reading for meaning. Of course you are. Meaning is compelling, and almost everything about reading trains your attention toward it.

But this book is written to intelligibility: to the part of you that must be able to grasp what is being said before meaning can do anything at all.

So we will not ask you to carry sentences whose intelligibility we have not settled ourselves.

Nothing is here merely because it sounds right. Every sentence has work to do.

That matters because this book is about something so natural that it is almost invisible:

the small structures through which meaning becomes clear enough to be yours.
